Questions

Honestly, two to four months for pages to rank. Ours started moving in about six weeks. The review replies and The Close work from the first day.

Square and Toast today. If you're on something else, ask. Most POS systems export what we need.

No. We add pages to the site you have. If we need a login, you give us one and take it back whenever you like.

Who actually does the work?

A small Miami team that runs the same systems in our own restaurants. You get one person to text, and it isn't a ticket queue.

Why is it priced so low for what's in it?

We built it once, for ourselves. Setting it up for you is mostly configuration, so it doesn't cost us a rebuild.
