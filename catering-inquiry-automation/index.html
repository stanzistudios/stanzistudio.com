The problem

One rule of catering: the job goes to whoever answers first.

Someone planning an office lunch or a rehearsal dinner sends the same request to several places. They book whoever comes back first with real answers. At our own restaurant, the leads we closed were the ones that got a real reply the same day.

A form that sits in a shared inbox over the weekend is a job handed to someone else.

How it works

Five steps from inquiry to calendar.

  1. 01Reply in minutes. From your own email address, in English or Spanish, at 11pm on a Sunday the same as at noon on a Tuesday.
  2. 02Ask what the quote needs. Date, headcount and budget up front, plus anything your prices depend on, like a delivery address or pickup.
  3. 03Draft the quote from your price list. Your packages, your per-person prices, your minimums. If a request doesn't fit the list, the desk doesn't guess. It holds the lead for you.
  4. 04Wait for your approval. The draft comes to you first. Change what you want, approve it, and it goes out.
  5. 05Follow up, then book. Leads that go quiet get a follow-up. When a job books, it lands on your calendar.

What comes to you

Four kinds of inquiry always come to you.

The desk does the typing and the chasing. Judgment calls stay with the owner.

  • Every quote. Drafted for you and sent only after you approve it.
  • Anything off your price list. A custom menu, a date you're closed, or a headcount above the limit you set.
  • Money. Deposits, refunds, discounts and payment terms.
  • Complaints, contracts and anything legal. An unhappy client, a contract to sign, or anyone mentioning a lawyer or the press.

Setup

Three things you give us on day one.

  1. 01Your catering price list. A PDF is fine. We turn it into rules the desk can read, and you check them before anything goes live.
  2. 02Access to the inbox your inquiries land in. You give us a login and take it back whenever you like. Your website and your form stay where they are.
  3. 03Your rules. How much notice you need, the biggest event you'll take without a call, where you deliver, and what you always want to see first.
