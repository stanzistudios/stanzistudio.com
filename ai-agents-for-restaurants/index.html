Who approves what

Two lists decide what an agent can do alone.

Before anything goes live, we write the rules down in plain English and you approve them along with the copy. The agent works inside them. Everything else comes to you.

The agent handles

  • First replies. Any hour, in English or Spanish, from your own address.
  • The questions a quote needs. Date, headcount and budget, asked up front.
  • Follow-ups. Every lead that goes quiet gets a nudge.
  • Good reviews. Four and five stars get a reply, posted on its own.
  • The paperwork. Invoices filed, statements checked, the 7am numbers sent.

Always comes to you

  • Quotes and prices. Drafted for you, sent only after you approve them.
  • Money. Refunds, discounts, deposits and payment terms.
  • Contracts. An agent never signs, accepts or changes one.
  • Angry guests. Complaints and reviews at three stars or under go to a person, the same day.
  • Anything legal. A lawyer, a reporter or a health complaint goes straight to the owner.

Questions

Nine things owners ask first.

What does an AI agent actually do in a restaurant?

One job, start to finish, inside rules you set. Ours answer catering inquiries, reply to reviews, file vendor invoices and send the morning numbers. They do the typing and the chasing. Anything with money, a contract or an upset guest comes to you.

How much do AI agents for restaurants cost?

Our prices are fixed and published. The review desk is $750 to set up and $200 a month per location. The catering inquiry desk is $1,500 plus $500 a month. Full Service for a whole group is $7,500 plus $2,000 a month. Custom builds start at $2,500.

Will an agent send a quote or a price without me?

No. Quotes are drafted from your price list and wait for your approval. Refunds, discounts, deposits and contracts always go to you.

How long does setup take?

About ten days, and about an hour of your time. The catering desk comes with a promise: live in 14 days or your first month is free.

Which systems does it work with?

Square and Toast for sales, your own email address for inquiries, and Google Sheets for anything you want to read. The invoice desk runs next to MarginEdge or xtraCHEF, or on its own.

Does it reply in Spanish?

Yes. Replies go out in English or Spanish, matching the person who wrote in.

Do you only work with Miami restaurants?

We're based in Miami and work across Miami-Dade, Broward and the rest of South Florida. We take on restaurants elsewhere in the US as well.

How do I leave?

Give 30 days' notice. There's no cancellation fee, and everything we built stays yours: the accounts, the sheets and the rules.

Who builds it?

A small Miami team that runs the same systems in our own restaurants and built Opstable, our restaurant workforce software. You get one person to text, not a ticket queue.
